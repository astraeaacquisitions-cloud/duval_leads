Sources: Duval County Clerk of Courts (Official Records) and the Duval County Tax Deed Auction (RealAuction). Property and mailing addresses cross-referenced against the Duval County Property Appraiser. Scores are a heuristic ranking, not a legal or financial assessment — verify every record before acting on it. Neither clerk system exposes a public deep link to the scanned document image; the "Record" links go to the relevant public search page or, for tax-deed items, straight to the parcel's Property Appraiser record.

Owner name caveat: the "Owner" field is the party named in the underlying court filing (e.g. the defendant on a Lis Pendens) — it reflects who is legally involved in that case, not necessarily who currently holds title. Title can move (marriage, divorce, an asset-protection transfer) faster than court and tax records catch up, so the current owner of record elsewhere (a skip-trace tool, the tax roll) can show a different name at the same address. Cross-check before reaching out; treat both names as connected to the property, not a mismatch to discard.
